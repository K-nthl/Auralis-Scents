REMINDERS & PRODUCT CARE

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

FAQs

Everything you need to know before ordering from Auralis Scents.

Are your perfumes authentic?

Yes. Auralis Scents offers authentic designer fragrances sourced from reputable suppliers. Each item is clearly described so customers know whether they are purchasing a factory mini or a decant.

Are your perfumes oil based?

No. Our fragrances are alcohol based perfumes, similar to the original fragrance they are taken from. The concentration and formulation may vary depending on the specific perfume.

Why is this so small and pricey?

Our minis may be small, but you're paying for the authentic designer fragrance inside, not simply the size of the bottle. Miniature and travel size bottles can also be more expensive per milliliter because they are smaller format branded products.

Do you ship nationwide?

Yes! We ship to customers across the Philippines through selected couriers. Shipping availability and rates may vary depending on your location.

Are these factory minis or decants?

It depends on the product. Some are original factory-made miniatures, while others are authentic fragrances carefully decanted into travel-size bottles. The type and exact volume are indicated in each product listing.

How do I place an order?

- Choose your preferred fragrance from our collection. - Click “Add to Cart.” - Review your selected items, then click “Place Order.” - After placing your order, click “Message US on Messenger” to inform us and confirm your order. - Wait for our confirmation and further instructions regarding payment and delivery.

How long does shipping take?

Metro Manila deliveries usually take 2–4 days, while provincial deliveries usually take 3–7 days after processing.

Do you accept returns or refunds?

Returns and refunds are applicable for damaged or incorrect deliveries. An unboxing video is required for verification.
