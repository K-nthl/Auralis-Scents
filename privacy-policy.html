Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 17, 2026

Auralis Scents respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the information you provide when placing an order or communicating with us.

1. Information We Collect

When you place an order through our website, we may collect information necessary to process and deliver your order, including:

  • Full name
  • Facebook or Messenger name
  • Phone number
  • Complete delivery address
  • ZIP code
  • Order details
  • Information related to payment and order confirmation

2. How We Use Your Information

The information you provide may be used to:

  • Process and record your order
  • Contact you regarding your order
  • Confirm delivery and payment details
  • Arrange shipping and delivery
  • Respond to questions or customer concerns
  • Maintain accurate order records

3. Order Information

Information submitted through the website's order form may be recorded in our order management system, including our connected Google Sheets system, for the purpose of processing and managing customer orders.

We only use this information for legitimate business and order-related purposes.

4. Messenger and Social Media

Auralis Scents may communicate with customers through Facebook Messenger or other social media channels when necessary to confirm orders, answer inquiries, or provide customer support.

Communications made through third-party platforms are also subject to the privacy policies and terms of those platforms.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell or intentionally share customer information for advertising or unrelated commercial purposes.

Information may be shared with selected delivery or courier services when necessary to fulfill and deliver an order.

6. Payment Information

Auralis Scents may accept payment through methods such as GCash, Maya, bank transfer, and selected Cash on Delivery options.

Customers should only provide payment information through the payment channels communicated by Auralis Scents. We do not request passwords, PINs, or one-time passwords (OTPs).

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect customer information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or misuse.

However, no method of storing or transmitting information over the internet can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention

Order information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary for order processing, customer service, business records, accounting, and other legitimate business purposes.

9. Your Privacy

If you have questions about the personal information you have provided to Auralis Scents, you may contact us through our official communication channels.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Auralis Scents may update this Privacy Policy when necessary to reflect changes in our services, order process, or business practices. Any updated version will be posted on this page.

11. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions or concerns, please contact Auralis Scents through our official Facebook Messenger page.
